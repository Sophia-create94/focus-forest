Applied only when the FAB is in pressed/active state. Not used as a surface fill. Sources its hue from the Moss accent token.
Why this matters — Glassmorphism is a layered technique, not a color. Documenting it as a flat hex causes flat green surfaces during developer handoff and breaks the visual hierarchy of the original design. The system describes the mechanism — translucent white linear gradient + backdrop-filter: blur(20px) over the --ff-primary canvas — via three surface tokens: --surface-header-nav, --surface-card, --surface-bottom-nav. Reference: Figma — Linear fill 30%, Background blur, Card radius 15px. Legacy --ff-green-header / --ff-green-card are kept only as deprecated fallbacks; do not use for new work.
