Rule — Link color always inherits from the surface context. Light surfaces (white, mist) use --color-link-on-light; dark surfaces (primary green, deep forest) use --color-link-on-dark. Both are Source Sans 3 Regular, 14px, underlined by default, and meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) on their intended background. The on-dark token reuses the existing Sky accent (#92D3F0).
