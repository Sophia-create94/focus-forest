Primary canvas 60%
The full-bleed Primary Green (#3B7A57) canvas, plus the glassmorphism progress card — still primary-green-derived because its translucent fill sits over the same canvas. This is the brand floor.
2
Light surfaces & text 30%
White (#FFFFFF) text — "Welcome!", "Your progress", "Good job!" — together with notification cards (on lock screen) and any other light surfaces. Provides breathing room and content legibility.
3
Accent punctuation 10%
Small, distributed moments — not one big block. The Yellow "See your jungle here" CTA (#F6AE2D), the active Home nav icon's yellow tint, and illustrative Sage/Cream notes inside the jungle scene (toucan beak, animal highlights). One CTA per screen; accents punctuate, never dominate.
The ratio rule. The 60-30-10 rule keeps Focus Forest visually grounded in its primary green identity (60%) with breathing room from light surfaces (30%) and energetic moments from accents (10%). Going beyond 10% on accents — for example, multiple yellow CTAs on one screen — breaks the visual hierarchy and makes the brand feel restless. Going below 30% on light surfaces makes screens feel heavy and reduces legibility for content.
